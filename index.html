Health IT Solution Prototype — SkinSync

A student-designed mobile health concept (SkinSync) exploring how remote care tools, structured data, and patient usability could work together to help people manage chronic skin conditions.

Learn more

SkinSync is a health IT concept I designed to improve remote care experiences for people managing chronic skin conditions through better data collection, clearer check-ins, and more approachable clinician-facing information. I built the wireframes and interface direction in Figma using a minimalist, mobile-first structure, with a focus on reducing friction so daily tracking feels simple and calm rather than overwhelming. The focus was not on shipping a production medical product, but on designing a more thoughtful system — one that considered patient input, structured health data, and the challenge of making remote interactions more useful. It helped me practice health informatics thinking, systems design, and translating healthcare problems into product concepts.

Calisthenics Kit

A hand-built weekly bodyweight training site with expandable day-by-day sessions, a stretching guide, and a program built around consistency over volume.

Learn more

Calisthenics Kit is a small static site I designed and built to turn a weekly bodyweight program into something easy to follow on any device. Each training day expands into warm-up, main work, and finisher blocks with sets, reps, and short coaching cues, alongside a stretching page and a list of non-negotiable habits, with a progress tracker planned next. I wrote it in plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with a dark, high-contrast visual system and deployed it on Netlify. The goal was an interface that stays fast, readable mid-workout, and honest about what actually matters: showing up every week.

Visit calisthenics-kit.netlify.app
